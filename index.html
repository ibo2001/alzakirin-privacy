Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · applies to Al Zakirin for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and its widgets.

Al Zakirin does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your reminder settings (start and end of your day, repeat interval, Do Not Disturb), favorites and tasbih count are stored only on your device, and shared with the app's own widgets through an App Group.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification is sent from a server.

Contacts

If you open the "Call a friend" good deed, the app asks for permission to read your contacts so it can suggest someone to call. Contacts are read on your device only when you use that feature. They are never uploaded, stored or shared.

Content from the internet

Hisn al-Muslim chapters and their recitations are downloaded from hisnmuslim.com. Like any website, that server can see the network request (for example your IP address). The app sends no personal information with these requests. Everything else in the app is included in the app itself.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

جهات الاتصال

عند فتح عمل الخير «اتصل بصديق» يطلب التطبيق إذنك لقراءة جهات الاتصال ليقترح عليك شخصًا تتصل به. تُقرأ جهات الاتصال على جهازك فقط عند استخدام هذه الميزة، ولا تُرفع ولا تُحفظ ولا تُشارك أبدًا.
